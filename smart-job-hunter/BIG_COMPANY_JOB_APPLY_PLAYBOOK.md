## Application Strategy (Daily)

### Daily Time Block (2.5 to 3 hours)
1. 20 min: Find fresh openings (last 24-72 hrs) on 10 target companies.
2. 50 min: Tailor resume for role cluster (Backend / Full Stack / SDET).
3. 60 min: Submit 6-8 high-quality direct applications.
4. 20 min: Request 3-5 referrals (alumni, peers, ex-colleagues).
5. 20 min: Follow up on applications sent 7-10 days ago.

### Daily KPI
- 6 to 8 direct company applications
- 3 to 5 referral requests
- 1 targeted recruiter outreach email
- 1 interview prep session (DSA/system design/project storytelling)

## Weekly Execution Plan

### Week 1 (Setup + Speed)
- Finalize 3 resume variants: Backend, Full Stack, SDET.
- Build master tracker with all 100 companies.
- Apply to first 35 openings.

### Week 2 (Scale)
- Apply to next 40 openings from top 60 companies.
- Send 20+ referral requests.
- Follow up on week 1 applications.

### Week 3 (Quality Boost)
- Focus on role-job fit score (skills overlap >70%).
- Improve project bullets with metrics.
- Apply to 30 high-fit roles.

### Week 4 (Conversion)
- Heavy follow-up on all pending applications.
- Interview prep sprints based on callbacks.
- Re-apply to newly opened matching roles.

## Resume Tailoring Rules (Fast)
- Mirror exact keywords from JD: Spring Boot, REST APIs, Microservices, Kafka, AWS, Docker, CI/CD.
- Keep top 40% of resume role-relevant.
- Add measurable impact in bullets (latency reduced %, cost saved, automation hours saved).
- Use one-page version for <6 years, two-page for senior profiles.
